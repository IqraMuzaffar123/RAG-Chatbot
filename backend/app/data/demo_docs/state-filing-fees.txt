# State Filing Fees — LLC and Corporation Formation Costs by State

## Overview

Every state charges a filing fee to form a Limited Liability Company (LLC) or Corporation. These fees vary significantly from state to state, ranging from as low as $40 to as high as $500 for initial formation. In addition to the formation fee, many states charge annual fees, franchise taxes, or report filing fees that represent ongoing costs of maintaining your business entity.

The information below covers the five most popular states for business formation. All fees are based on publicly available information from state government websites and are subject to change.

## LLC Formation Fees by State

### Texas
- **Formation filing fee:** $300
- **Filed with:** Texas Secretary of State
- **Document:** Certificate of Formation
- **Online filing available:** Yes
- **Expedited processing:** Available for an additional $25 (24-hour processing)
- **Annual report fee:** No annual report fee; however, the annual Franchise Tax Report and Public Information Report must be filed with the Texas Comptroller (no separate filing fee)
- **Name reservation fee:** $40 (reserves name for 120 days)

### California
- **Formation filing fee:** $70
- **Filed with:** California Secretary of State
- **Document:** Articles of Organization (Form LLC-1)
- **Online filing available:** Yes
- **Expedited processing:** Available for an additional fee ($350 for same-day, $750 for 4-hour processing)
- **Biennial Statement of Information fee:** $20 (due every two years)
- **Annual franchise tax:** $800 minimum (due to the Franchise Tax Board, separate from the Secretary of State filing)
- **Name reservation fee:** $10 (reserves name for 60 days)

### Delaware
- **Formation filing fee:** $90
- **Filed with:** Delaware Division of Corporations
- **Document:** Certificate of Formation
- **Online filing available:** Yes
- **Expedited processing:** Available — same-day ($100 additional), 24-hour ($50 additional)
- **Annual tax:** $300 (due June 1 each year)
- **Name reservation fee:** $75 (reserves name for 120 days)

### New York
- **Formation filing fee:** $200
- **Filed with:** New York Department of State
- **Document:** Articles of Organization
- **Online filing available:** Yes
- **Expedited processing:** Available for an additional $25 (24-hour processing)
- **Biennial statement fee:** $9 (due every two years)
- **Publication requirement cost:** Approximately $1,000 to $2,000+ (varies by county; LLCs must publish notice in two newspapers for six consecutive weeks within 120 days of formation)
- **Name reservation fee:** $20 (reserves name for 60 days)

### Florida
- **Formation filing fee:** $125
- **Filed with:** Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz)
- **Document:** Articles of Organization
- **Online filing available:** Yes (required — Florida requires online filing)
- **Expedited processing:** Not available; standard processing is typically 2-3 business days
- **Annual report fee:** $138.75 (due May 1 each year)
- **Late filing fee:** $400 (if filed after May 1)
- **Name reservation fee:** $25 (reserves name for 120 days)

## Corporation Formation Fees by State

### Texas
- **Formation filing fee:** $300
- **Filed with:** Texas Secretary of State
- **Document:** Certificate of Formation
- **Annual franchise tax:** Based on margin; no tax due if total revenue is at or below $2.47 million

### California
- **Formation filing fee:** $100
- **Filed with:** California Secretary of State
- **Document:** Articles of Incorporation
- **Annual Statement of Information fee:** $25
- **Annual franchise tax:** $800 minimum

### Delaware
- **Formation filing fee:** $89
- **Filed with:** Delaware Division of Corporations
- **Document:** Certificate of Incorporation
- **Annual report fee:** $50
- **Annual franchise tax:** Minimum $225 (calculated based on authorized shares or assumed par value capital)

### New York
- **Formation filing fee:** $125
- **Filed with:** New York Department of State
- **Document:** Certificate of Incorporation
- **Biennial statement fee:** $9

### Florida
- **Formation filing fee:** $70
- **Filed with:** Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz)
- **Document:** Articles of Incorporation
- **Annual report fee:** $150

## Additional Cost Considerations

When budgeting for business formation, consider these additional costs beyond the state filing fee:

- **Registered agent service:** $100 to $300 per year if using a professional service
- **Operating agreement or bylaws:** $0 if self-drafted; $500 to $2,000 if prepared by an attorney
- **EIN application:** Free (apply directly through IRS.gov)
- **Business licenses and permits:** Vary widely by location and industry ($50 to $500+)
- **Business insurance:** General liability insurance typically starts at $300 to $1,000 per year
- **Business bank account:** Many banks offer free business checking; some require minimum balances
- **Accounting and tax preparation:** $500 to $3,000+ per year depending on complexity
- **Legal consultation:** $150 to $400+ per hour for business attorney services

## Tips for Reducing Formation Costs

- File online whenever possible — online filing is often faster and sometimes cheaper than paper filing
- Apply for your EIN directly through IRS.gov — it is free; avoid third-party services that charge fees
- Consider whether you truly need to form in a different state — forming in Delaware or Nevada when you operate in another state means paying fees in both states
- Use free or low-cost templates for operating agreements and bylaws when appropriate
- Compare registered agent service prices — they range from $49 to $300+ per year
